    Check trucks in at a door, unload their boxes, and send each box to a process station.

    Process.

    Open each box and check every item. Clean items go in a yellow tote to be sold. Damaged items go to liquidation.

    Tranship.

    Stage full yellow totes, load them onto the outbound truck, and ship them to be sold.

    Liquidation.

    Damaged items collect on the pallet until it ships to the liquidation partner.
